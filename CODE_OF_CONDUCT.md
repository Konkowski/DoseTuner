# Code of Conduct

We want DoseTuner to be a welcoming, practical community for people who run and improve filling machines.

**Do:** be respectful and constructive; assume good intent; give specific, evidence-based feedback; credit others' work; keep discussion on the technical topic.

**Don't:** harass, insult or discriminate; post private or confidential data (machine data must be anonymised); pressure others.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions and block people who break these rules. Report problems through a private GitHub security/contact channel on the maintainer's profile or via a private Discussion request. This text is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant 2.1.
